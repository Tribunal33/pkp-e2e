## Summary

**Finding 1 is fixed**, and nothing is left open. A manager can delete a
stage's "Discussion (…)" template under Settings › Workflow › "Tasks and
Discussions". On such a stage, a message typed in "Notify" or "Assign"
with no predefined message chosen now goes out the same way as on any
other stage:

- the window closes with "Notification sent to users.";
- one discussion opens;
- the recipient gets an email titled "Discussion (Submission)"
  ("Discussion (Production)" on OPS), with the typed text and the
  discussion footer: "Reply to this comment at #{id} {authors} or
  unsubscribe … from emails sent by {journal}".

Seen on OJS, OMP and OPS. The new commit `e39fdee199` gives the fallback
email the `Discussion` trait and the submission and context, so its
unsubscribe footer is available. Round 3 proposed building the fallback
from the new discussion instead. Either approach works.

Every fix from the earlier rounds still holds:

- the blank list sends under the stage's "Discussion (…)";
- the discussion lists its sender as creator;
- templates added in Settings, limited or not, are sent under their own
  names;
- the list set back to blank empties "Message";
- a press's Internal Review offers "Discussion (Review)", and so does a
  press upgraded from 3.5.

The suites run at the PR heads on all three apps (Evidence).
