# With the masthead or its reviewer list switched off, invitations and role changes still tell people their name will appear on the masthead

Intention gap. OJS, OMP and OPS at ojs `9d9f116f38`, omp `480045c32`,
ops `5da5bc48ad` (lib/pkp `fab29cfeca`); the change pkp/pkp-lib#13370
(`fab29cfeca`, 2026-09-28, "Port masthead configuration improvements to
main") leaves the invitation and role-change emails out.
stable-3_5_0: shows it too at lib/pkp `8809a197de` (the same settings
since the 3.5 twin, 2026-09-24). Tracked in ci-triage "Open regressions"
and the journal identity spec (U07 A13). Temporary: delete once
acted on.

## Summary

Issue #13370 lets a journal switch off its enrollment-based masthead
(off on every journal upgraded to this version) and makes the list of
last year's reviewers opt-in, because reviewer names "may reveal
information … the journal does not wish to reveal". The screens and
emails that tell a person whether they will be listed were not changed:
with the masthead off, "Invite to a role" still offers "Appear on the
masthead" and its email says "Your name will appear in the <journal>'s
masthead as a Section editor."; a role's masthead change on "Edit user"
still mails "New setting: Appear on the masthead"; and every reviewer
invitation says "Your name will appear … as a Reviewer." although no
journal lists reviewers unless a manager turns that on.

## Impact

Every journal, press and server upgraded to this version (their
enrollment masthead reads off until a manager ticks it), and every new
one for reviewers. The people invited or changed get an email promising
a public listing that the journal does not show; for reviewers, the email
contradicts the privacy reason the issue gives for hiding them. Managers
see a masthead choice on "Invite to a role" and "Edit user" that does
nothing while the masthead is off. No data is lost. Minor: misleading
wording in two emails and on two screens, but on every upgraded site.

## Steps to reproduce

Preconditions:
- A fresh OJS install with a journal, its Journal Manager, a Section
  editor listed on the masthead, and an existing Author and Reader
  account.

1. As a visitor open About › "Editorial Masthead": the Section editor is
   listed.
2. As the Journal Manager open Settings › Website › "Appearance" ›
   "Editorial Masthead", untick "Present a masthead based on user
   enrollments" and press "Save" (the state an upgraded journal starts
   in).
3. Open Settings › Users & Roles › "Invite to a role", search the
   Author's email, add the role "Section editor" from today with "Appear
   on the masthead", continue and press "Invite user to the role".
4. Read the invitation email the Author received.
5. On Users & Roles open the Section editor's "Edit", set the held
   "Section editor" role to "Does not appear on the masthead", confirm,
   then back to "Appear on the masthead", confirm. Read the second email.
6. As a visitor reload About › "Editorial Masthead".
7. For reviewers: on a new journal (the masthead on, "Enable listing of
   reviewers on the masthead" unticked by default), invite the Reader to
   "Reviewer" and read the email.

**Expected**: with the masthead off, the screens do not offer a masthead
choice (or say it has no effect) and the emails do not promise a
listing; a reviewer invitation does not promise a listing the journal
does not show.

**Observed**: step 3's role row still reads "Journal Masthead: Appear on
the masthead / Does not appear on the masthead" and the wizard's header
"…along with appearing in the journal masthead". Step 4's email reads
"Your name will appear in the <journal>'s masthead as a Section editor."
Step 5's confirmation reads "This will update whether this user appears
on the journal masthead for the selected role. The user will be notified
of this change." and the email "Your journal masthead visibility for the
role Section editor (since September 29, 2026) in <journal> has been
updated. New setting: Appear on the masthead". Step 6's page shows
"Editorial Masthead" and no one. Step 7's row shows the fixed text
"Appear on the masthead" and the email reads "Your name will appear in
the <journal>'s masthead as a Reviewer."; the public page has no "Peer
Reviewers in Previous Year".

## Cause

Only `AboutContextHandler` reads the new `enableEnrollmentMasthead` and
`enableEnrollmentMastheadReviewers` settings. The invitation email
(`UserRoleAssignmentInvitationNotify`, the `userGroupSectionWillAppear`
line), the role-change email (`UserRoleMastheadUpdateNotify::setData()`,
sent from `PKPUserController::masthead()`), and the ui-library's
invitation role table (the reviewer row fixed to "Appear on the
masthead", "since they are always displayed") still assume every masthead
role and every reviewer is listed.

## Proposed fix

A proposal: have both mailables and the invitation role table read the
context's two settings. With the enrollment masthead off, drop the "Your
name will appear…" line and the masthead column (or mark it as having no
effect), and send no masthead-visibility email; for a reviewer role,
promise a listing only when "Enable listing of reviewers on the masthead"
is ticked. A smaller step: reword the emails to "may appear on the
masthead, if the journal displays one".
