## Summary

`pkp/ui-library#982` makes the progress box under "Add" count every
reference, which fixes the miscount of `pkp/pkp-lib#13308` for references
just added: "0/5", "2/5", "All 5 references successfully processed".
But a reference that no lookup was ever asked for never finishes. Those
added before lookup was switched on, and those upgraded from 3.5, keep
the box at "Processing references - 0/n" for good, and the page
re-fetches the submission and the publication every 7 seconds while it is
open. That is the symptom `pkp/pkp-lib#12155` asked to remove and
`pkp/ui-library#876` removed; `main` shows no box over them today.

## Impact

- **Lost** Nothing stored. The editor is told that a lookup is running
  over references no lookup will ever touch, and each open tab sends two
  requests every 7 seconds for as long as it stays open.
- **Who** Editors and anyone who may edit a publication's metadata, on
  the "References" page, on every journal, press and server that
  switches on "Enable references structuring and metadata lookup" (off
  by default) while its publications already hold references: every
  install upgraded from 3.5, and any context that turns lookup on later.
  The native import with `citation-metadata-lookup` off leaves
  references the same way (code read).
- **Way round** "Reprocess all references" asks a lookup for each, after
  which the box counts down and finishes; it also replaces details
  filled in by hand (U42 Rule 15).

Low: nothing is lost and every row is right; only the box and its
refresh mislead. What would raise it is the reach: on an upgraded install
the box would stand on nearly every publication that has references.

## Steps to reproduce

Preconditions: PKP's default test dataset for `main` (OJS, OMP or OPS),
with the three PRs checked out (ojs `1f7aa2eca5`, lib/pkp `14c8d75c50`,
lib/ui-library `2e330ff4`, frontend rebuilt). Lookup is off in the
dataset, and no submission has a reference.

1. Sign in as `dbarnes` (password `dbarnesdbarnes`).
2. Open submission 4, "Computer Skill Requirements for New and Existing
   Teachers: Implications for Policy and Practice" (OJS), 3, "The
   Political Economy of Workplace Injury in Canada" (OMP), or 1, "The
   influence of lactation on the quantity and quality of cashmere
   production" (OPS), then "Publication" ("Preprint" on OPS) ›
   "References".
3. Type these two lines into the "References" box and press "Add":

   ```
   u42r7 Earlier one. Test Press; 2019.
   u42r7 Earlier two. Test Press; 2019.
   ```

4. Open "Settings" › "Workflow" › "Metadata", tick "Enable references
   structuring and metadata lookup" and press "Save".
5. Open the submission's "References" again, look under the "Add" box,
   and watch the browser's network panel for 30 seconds.

**Expected.** No box under "Add", and no requests: no lookup was asked
for either reference (`pkp/pkp-lib#12155`; what `main` shows today).

**Observed.** The same on the three apps: "Processing references - 0/2"
with "We're retrieving metadata for each reference. This may take a few
moments. …", and `GET submissions/<id>` with `GET
submissions/<id>/publications/<id>` every 7 seconds (three pairs in 22
seconds). It stays so after one of them is filled in by hand ("Edit":
DOI, Title, an author) and after "Reprocess" on the other. References
with no stored status, as an upgrade from 3.5 leaves them (the REST API
gives `processingStatus: null`), show the same.

Control, the issue's own case: five references added with lookup on
read "Processing references - 0/5", "2/5" with two finished, and "All 5
references successfully processed" with all five, and the page refreshed
itself until then.

## Cause

`citationManagerStore.js` at the PR head counts `totalCitations` as every
citation and `finishedCitations` as those whose `processingStatus` is
`PROCESSED` or `FAILED`; the box shows while `totalCitations > 0`, and
the 7-second refresh runs while lookup is on and `!isFinished`. A
reference no lookup was asked for keeps `NOT_PROCESSED` (0), or no status
at all after an upgrade from 3.5, and so never counts as finished.

Before the PR the store counted structured references only. That was
`pkp/ui-library#876`'s stand-in for "references a lookup was asked for"
(`pkp/pkp-lib#12155`), and it caused the miscount `pkp/pkp-lib#13308`
reports. Neither count can tell the two apart, because the stored status
cannot: `PKP\citation\Repository::importCitations()` stores
`NOT_PROCESSED` whether or not it then queues a lookup, and no migration
writes a status for references from 3.5.

## Proposed fix

The fix proposed in [pkp-e2e#883](https://github.com/jardakotesovec/pkp-e2e/issues/883)
(the team's report of the miscount,
`docs/issues/U42-A6-reference-lookup-progress-counts-structured-only.md`,
"Proposed fix"), on top of this PR: `Repository::reprocessCitation()`,
the one place a lookup is queued, stores a new status `QUEUED` (-2)
before dispatching the chain, and the store counts only references whose
status is `QUEUED`, a lookup stage (`PID_EXTRACTED` to `PROCESSED`), or,
with this PR, `FAILED`. `NOT_PROCESSED` and no status stay out, so the
box and the refresh leave them alone. This PR's `FAILED` handling, its
badge and its "{processed} of {total} references processed, {failed}
incomplete" wording fit as they are. That fix was tried on `main` on the
three apps (2026-10-04): a fresh "Add" counted as here, and references
added before lookup was on showed no box and no refresh.

Medium: two repositories (pkp-lib and ui-library), a new status value in
the REST API's `processingStatus`, and the jobs' `failed()` shared so an
abandoned chain stores `FAILED` (detail in the issue report).
